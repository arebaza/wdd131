Travel Planning Notes

  • Lima: Great food, museums, and ocean views.
  • Cusco + Sacred Valley: High altitude—hydrate and plan acclimation.
  • Arequipa: A beautiful “white city” with access to Colca Canyon.
  • Machu Picchu: Book early and pack light.
