Peru Travel Guide — Website Planning Document

WDD 131 – Project Site Plan

1. Site Name

Peru Travel Guide

This name was chosen because the site focuses on helping visitors discover Peru through its most important attractions: places to visit, traditional food, and historical periods. The name is simple, clear, and easy to remember for travelers who want practical and cultural information.

Optional domain example: perutravelguide.example

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of the Peru Travel Guide website is to provide visitors with helpful information about traveling in Peru. The site will present recommended places to visit, famous Peruvian foods, and a simple history timeline that can be explored by year range. It will also include a contact form so users can share their travel interests. The site is designed for students, tourists, and anyone who wants a basic cultural and travel overview of Peru.

3. Scenarios

  • What are the best places to visit in Peru if I only have a few days?
  • Which traditional Peruvian foods should I try during my trip?
  • How can I learn about different periods of Peruvian history in a simple way?
  • How can I contact the site to share my travel interests or ask questions?

5. Typography

The site will use two main fonts:

  • Montserrat – Used for headings and titles to create strong visual hierarchy.
  • Open Sans – Used for body text, paragraphs, and form content for readability.

These fonts are clean, modern, and easy to read on both mobile and desktop screens.
