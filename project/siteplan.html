Peru Travel Guide

Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

Peru Travel Guide

This name was chosen because it clearly represents a website dedicated to providing travel information, cultural highlights, and destination guidance for visitors interested in Peru.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of this site is to help travelers learn about Peru by offering information on popular destinations, cultural traditions, weather, and travel tips to help visitors plan their trips effectively.

3. Scenarios

  • What are the most popular places to visit in Peru?
  • What is the best time of year to travel to Peru?
