Peru Travel Guide — Website Planning Document

WDD 131 – W05 Project Site Plan

1. Site Name

Peru Travel Guide

This name was chosen because the website focuses on helping visitors discover Peru through places to visit, traditional food, and a history timeline. The name is simple, clear, and easy to remember for travelers.

Optional domain example: perutravelguide.example

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of the Peru Travel Guide website is to provide helpful information for visitors who want to travel in Peru. The site presents recommended places to visit, famous Peruvian foods, and a simple history timeline that can be explored by year range. It also includes a contact form so users can share their travel interests and questions.

3. Scenarios

  • What are the best places to visit in Peru if I only have a few days?
  • Which traditional Peruvian foods should I try during my trip?
  • How can I learn about different periods of Peruvian history in a simple way?
  • How can I contact the site to share my travel interests or ask questions?
